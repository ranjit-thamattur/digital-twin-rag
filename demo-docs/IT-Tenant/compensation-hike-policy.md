# Employee Compensation & Salary Revision Policy 2026
## Internal Policy: Admin IT Solutions

### Annual Salary Increment (Hike) Framework
Salary revisions are conducted annually in Q1 based on performance evaluations from the previous fiscal year.

### Performance Categories & Hike Percentages
Increments are determined by the following performance ratings:

| Rating | Category | Hike Range | Criteria |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **P5** | Exceptional | 15% - 25% | Exceeded all KPIs; demonstrated significant leadership/innovation. |
| **P4** | High Performer | 10% - 14% | Exceeded most KPIs; consistently high impact on projects. |
| **P3** | Solid Achiever | 5% - 9% | Met all primary KPIs; standard expected contribution. |
| **P2** | Needs Improvement | 0% - 4% | Met some KPIs but missed critical milestones; requires PIP. |
| **P1** | Unsatisfactory | 0% | Failed to meet base requirements. |

### Approval Workflow for Salary Increments
To maintain budget discipline, the following approval hierarchy is mandatory:
1.  **Direct Manager:** Proposes hike based on performance review.
2.  **Department Head (VP/CTO):** Reviews for parity across the team.
3.  **HR Business Partner (HRBP):** Validates against market benchmarks and budget caps.
4.  **Finance Department:** Performs final budget verification.
5.  **CEO/Managing Director:** Final signature required for any hike above 20%.

### Out-of-Cycle Increments (Midsession Hikes)
Midsession hikes are only permitted under the following conditions:
*   **Promotion:** Change in role or significant increase in responsibilities.
*   **Retention:** Counter-offer situation for key 'A-player' talent (requires CTO approval).
*   **Market Correction:** Adjusting salary to meet industry standards for specialized technical roles.

### Confidentiality Clause
Specific salary revision details must not be shared between employees. Breach of confidentiality may lead to disciplinary action.
